# MVP Scope & Feature Backlog 
## The Ultimate Product Vision
- OwlDoit serves as a more efficient and lightweight alternative that combines all the needs of a student to support his academic studies in college.
## The Launch Goal (v1.0 MVP)
To launch a functional version by 2035 focusing strictly on core value.
## Feature Prioritization Matrix
*   **Must Have (MVP Lineup):**
    *   All-in-One college planner: This feature serves as the overall core of the application, making sure the student is up to date on their tasks and for any upcoming events or homework
    *   In-app checklist and notepad: It helps the student keep track of their homework, letting them order them by date or priority, and also letting them write notes for each course
    *   Optimized Browsing: Since everything is in one application, the student won't need to open multiple tabs or apps anymore to keep track of their studies, while simultaneously keeping the overall app as lightweight as possible, with options allowing to lessen the load even more for low-end PCs
