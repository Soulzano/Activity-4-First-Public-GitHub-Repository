FEATURES:

#1 [All-in-one College Planner]
- OwlDoit can function as a schedule planner for setting reminders for each class, a calendar for campus events that you are interested in, and even as a course planner for future semesters.

#2 [Links Google account to your school email/app]
- Alongside being an all-in-one planner, you can link both your Google account and School account like Canvas, so any changes you make in Google calendar for example will also affect your canvas calendar and vice-versa, making it an easy way to track and plan for classes or events.

#3 [Optimized browsing for low-end PCs]
- Due to being a singular app with lots of features, for students with low-end PCs, this application has low memory consumption when compared to opening multiple apps or tabs in a browser when OwlDoit does all of it in one singular application. 

#4 [Built in Microsoft Word]
- OwlDoit has its own built-in Microsoft Word like feature, where you can make essays and edit homework just like the app, but does not have the other Microsoft Office applications like Excel and Powerpoint, which makes it strictly for essay or text editing.

#5 [Features an in-app checklist and notepad]
- Lastly, OwlDoit has a small notepad icon that you can access to write quick notes for each class, and a checklist to keep your homework in check to prevent late submissions and missed assignments.
