Application Name: OwlDoit
Owldoit is an application made for college students to have all their tasks in one app instead of
switching every time they have to adjust their schedules or see their grades. It links your google
account and school email in the app, now it prevents the hassle of switching apps when
performing tasks. Since it links both accounts into one, you can freely use word or google drive
while simultaneously checking which courses you have and what grades are shown, turning a
20-minute hassle into a 10-minute task. It especially helps those with a low end pc and cant
open multiple applications at once.
